What the sample shows

Within this constructed dataset, recorded monthly revenue rises from $18,200 in January 2024 to $34,700 in December 2025. The calculated operating margin rises from 1.4% to 14.0% across those two endpoints. These are descriptions of the sample’s designed values only; they do not establish a real growth trend or explain why a change occurred.

Method and assumptions

  • Dataset: download the synthetic monthly CSV, created for this learning project. It contains 24 rows from January 2024 through December 2025.
  • Amounts are illustrative USD values. Revenue is a constructed sequence; cost of goods sold and operating expenses are generated using explicit deterministic formulas documented in README.md.
  • Gross profit = revenue − cost of goods sold. Simplified operating profit = gross profit − listed operating expenses. Operating margin = simplified operating profit ÷ revenue.
  • No external sources, company records, customers, or survey results were used. No currency conversion, seasonal adjustment, inflation adjustment, or statistical inference was performed.

Limitations and next steps

The numbers are intentionally synthetic. Their pattern reflects how the sample was constructed, not evidence about a business. There is no basis here for causal explanations, forecasts, benchmarking, or management recommendations.

With a documented real dataset, the next iteration would validate definitions and missing values, compare monthly and year-over-year results, investigate cost drivers, and present a decision question with appropriate context. For now, this project demonstrates a transparent calculation workflow and clear communication of limitations.
